PER by condition

Transitions between behaviors

s

Each bee's bouts are put in order of start time; each bout followed by the next is one transition. A cell is the chance that the row behavior is followed by the column behavior, pooled over the selected bees. Idle marks a gap with no behavior. Count repeats keeps a behavior followed by itself.

p-values use the Mann–Whitney U test for two groups and the Kruskal–Wallis test for three or more (per behavior, bees as replicates), and Fisher's exact test (two groups) or a chi-square test for PER. They are exploratory and not corrected for multiple comparisons. Export the CSVs for formal analysis.
